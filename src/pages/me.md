Some of my patients need medicine. Others need a good listener. I provide both.

I work in primary care as a medical assistant. I’m also studying to become an
RN. In my copious spare time, I design & build web apps and client sites for the
digitally challenged. I like to think I’m still changing the world, but in a
different way.

When I’m not solving the world’s problems, I’m playing the piano or reading a
fantasy novel. I also read my email — and sometimes respond.
